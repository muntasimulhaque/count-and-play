# Count & Play: Play Console Reference (v7 era)

Paste-ready material for the Play Console listing and release flow. The v1
submission pack (PWABuilder zip, Netlify TWA) and the v6 listing text are
history: everything below describes the native app as it actually ships.

## Irreplaceable

`signing.keystore` + `signing-key-info.txt` (vault folder outside the repo,
base64 copy in the `KEYSTORE_BASE64` GitHub secret). If they are lost the app
can never be updated again. Keep copies in two places.

## Store art

All store art is generated by `tools/make_art.py` in the app's own design
language: the flat warm ground, the candy palette with each hue's darker
outline, hard facets instead of gradients, and the real vector shapes from
`ShapeArt.kt`. Regenerate with `python tools/make_art.py`; never edit the PNGs
by hand.

- `play-store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`: title, two apples plus three
  carrots wearing their count chips 1 2 / 1 2 3, and the tagline.
- `play-store/play-icon-512.png`: white plate on the candy blue, ball, apple
  and carrot.
- The launcher icons (`mipmap-*/ic_launcher.png` and
  `mipmap-*/ic_launcher_foreground.png`) come from the same trio, so the
  shelf and the home screen always match.

## Store listing (v7)

**App name** (30 chars max): `Count & Play`

**Short description** (80 chars max):

```
See addition and subtraction happen. Three tap-to-play games. No ads, offline.
```

**Full description**:

```
Count & Play helps a very young child SEE what numbers are, and what adding
and taking away do to them. Three big picture games, each chosen with one tap:

• Count them: tap each object once, hear its number, and a numbered chip stays
  on it. The chips follow your child's own tapping order, because any order is
  a right order.

• Put together: two plates are counted on their own, then your child pours
  them into one bowl with one big button, and counts what that made. The parts
  keep their colours inside the whole, so five still reads as three-and-two at
  a glance.

• Take away: "take away two!" Each tapped piece wears its number as it sinks
  into its ghost, and your child counts what is left. Left is a quantity you
  can see, not a disappearing act.

Every round ends with the fact arriving huge on screen, 3 + 2 = 5, while the
voice says it, then confetti. Difficulty creeps up invisibly inside each game
and eases whenever your child struggles. There is no score, no timer, and no
fail state: the word wrong is never spoken.

Made for little learners:
• Everything is spoken aloud, in English and বাংলা, so no reading is needed
• Huge touch targets made for small fingers
• No music, no characters: just shapes, colour, motion and voice
• No ads, no purchases, no data collection, zero permissions, ever
• Fully offline after the first open

Made by a parent, for parents who want to sit with their child and teach.
```

## Release path

- Production is the public track; alpha keeps running for testers. Managed
  publishing is off, so an approved release publishes itself.
- Every release: bump versionCode and versionName, push `main`, CI signs and
  publishes the AAB to the `latest-build` GitHub release (pull it from there),
`gh run download` the screenshot artifacts into `store-shots/`, then Play
Console: create the release, paste English-only notes, attach screenshots.
- The app is fully native and fully offline: every change, however small, needs
  a new build and a Play release. Nothing ships without it.
